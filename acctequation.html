Key Formulas

Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity
Beginning Capital + Investments + Net Income − Withdrawals = Ending Capital
Net Income = Total Revenue − Total Expenses
  • Type 1 — Capital Word Problems: Multi-year scenarios. Calculate net income, ending capital, and solve for missing amounts (like total withdrawals).
  • Type 2 — Transaction Analysis: Identify transactions from accounting equation changes using UIL letter codes (A–K).

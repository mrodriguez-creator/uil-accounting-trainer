Income Statement Formula Chain

UIL tests require you to calculate these step-by-step:

Sales − Sales Discounts − Sales R&A = Net Sales
Purchases + Transportation In − Purchases Discounts − Purchases R&A = Net Purchases
Net Purchases = Cost of Delivered Merchandise (same thing)
Beginning Inventory + Cost of Delivered Merch = Cost of Merch Available for Sale
Cost of Merch Available − Ending Inventory = Cost of Merchandise Sold
Net Sales − Cost of Merch Sold = GROSS PROFIT

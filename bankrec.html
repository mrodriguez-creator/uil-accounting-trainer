Bank Reconciliation Formula

Both sides must equal the same Adjusted (Reconciled) Balance:

BANK SIDE
Bank Statement Balance
+ Deposits in Transit
− Outstanding Checks
= Adjusted Bank Balance
BOOK SIDE
Checkbook Balance
− Bank Service Charge
− Other Bank Charges
= Adjusted Book Balance

Adjusted Bank Balance MUST EQUAL Adjusted Book Balance

Petty Cash Fund Formulas

UIL tests use two main petty cash problem types:

PETTY CASH FUND
Opening Balance
+ Increase in Fund (if any)
Fund Total
− Vouchers Totaling
= Expected Cash
Actual Cash Before Replenishment
Actual − Expected = Cash Over/<Short>
Fund Total − Actual Cash = Replenishment Amount
CHANGE FUND / CASH DRAWER
Cash Sales
+ Sales Tax Collected
+ Collected on Account
+ Change Fund
= Expected Cash in Drawer
Actual Cash in Drawer
Actual − Expected = Cash Over/<Short>

Shortages are shown in angle brackets: <$2.15>

UIL Worksheet Columns

Given an account with a normal balance, select the worksheet column it extends to:

A. Income Statement DEBIT

Expenses, COGS accounts, contra-Revenue (Sales Discounts, Sales R&A), Transportation In

B. Income Statement CREDIT

Revenue (Sales, Fees Earned), contra-COGS (Purchases Discounts, Purchases R&A)

C. Balance Sheet DEBIT

Assets (Cash, Supplies, Equipment), Drawing

D. Balance Sheet CREDIT

Liabilities, Owner's Capital, contra-Assets (Accum. Depr., AUA)

E. Not Extended

Income Summary (adjustments only), Beginning Merchandise Inventory (adjusted via Income Summary)

C-DID — Order of Closing Entries

UIL tests use this exact coding system. Know which accounts close and where:

A DR — Closed into Income Summary (Debit)

Expenses, COGS, Transportation In, contra-Revenue (Sales Discounts, Sales R&A)

A CR — Closed into Income Summary (Credit)

Revenue (Sales, Fees), contra-COGS (Purchases Discounts, Purchases R&A)

B DR — Closed into Capital (Debit)

Income Summary with Net Loss (debit balance)

B CR — Closed into Capital (Credit)

Income Summary with Net Income, Owner's Drawing

C — Not Closed (Permanent Accounts)

Assets, Liabilities, Owner's Capital — A=L+OE accounts carry forward

C-DID Closing Order: 1) Credits → IS, 2) Debits → IS, 3) Income Summary → Capital, 4) Drawing → Capital

Income Statement Formula Chain

UIL tests require you to calculate these step-by-step:

Sales − Sales Discounts − Sales R&A = Net Sales
Purchases + Transportation In − Purchases Discounts − Purchases R&A = Net Purchases
Net Purchases = Cost of Delivered Merchandise (same thing)
Beginning Inventory + Cost of Delivered Merch = Cost of Merch Available for Sale
Cost of Merch Available − Ending Inventory = Cost of Merchandise Sold
Net Sales − Cost of Merch Sold = GROSS PROFIT

Bank Reconciliation Formula

Both sides must equal the same Adjusted (Reconciled) Balance:

BANK SIDE
Bank Statement Balance
+ Deposits in Transit
− Outstanding Checks
= Adjusted Bank Balance
BOOK SIDE
Checkbook Balance
− Bank Service Charge
− Other Bank Charges
= Adjusted Book Balance

Adjusted Bank Balance MUST EQUAL Adjusted Book Balance

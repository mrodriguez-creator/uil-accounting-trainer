Key Formulas

Sales Tax = Sale Price × Tax Rate
Customer Total = Sale Price + Sales Tax
Sales Tax Payable = Total tax collected − Tax already paid to state
  • Bank card sales: Treated like cash sales — Cash is debited for the total (sale + tax).
  • Sales on account: Accounts Receivable is debited for the total (sale + tax).
  • Paying the state: Debit Sales Tax Payable, Credit Cash for the amount owed.

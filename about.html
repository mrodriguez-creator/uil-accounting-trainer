Built for student success

✅ What's Working Now

  • 8 Complete Lessons: Account Classification, Journal Entries, Closing Entries, GAAP Concepts, Worksheet Columns, Gross Profit, Bank Reconciliation, Payroll Fundamentals
  • 21 Interactive Flashcards: Click to flip and study acronyms
  • 10-Question Quiz: Instant grading with explanations
  • 25-Question True/False: Real UIL test questions
  • Debit/Credit Practice: 20 random from 99 questions (normal balances, increase/decrease, transactions)
  • Worksheet Columns: 20 random from 42 accounts — extend to IS/BS columns like real UIL tests
  • GAAP Concepts Matching: 15 random from 40+ questions — match descriptions to concepts, principles & assumptions
  • Closing Codes Quiz: 15 random from 40+ questions — permanent vs temporary, C-DID closing entries with exact UIL codes
  • Gross Profit Calculator: Step-by-step income statement chain — Net Sales, Net Purchases, COGS, Gross Profit with random scenarios
  • Bank Reconciliation: Reconcile bank & book sides — deposits in transit, outstanding checks, service charges with random scenarios
  • Petty Cash Practice: Petty cash fund & change fund problems — vouchers, replenishment, cash over/short with random scenarios
  • Payroll Practice: 3 problem types — gross pay (hourly+OT), employer payroll tax expense (SS/Med/FUTA/SUTA with wage caps), entry classification (SE-E/PTE-R)
  • Progress Tracking: Points, accuracy, and stats that update!

Four Adjustment Types

  • Prepaid Insurance: Debit Insurance Expense, Credit Prepaid Insurance (for months expired)
  • Supplies: Debit Supplies Expense, Credit Supplies (amount used = TB balance − physical count)
  • Merchandise Inventory: Adjusted through Income Summary. Increase: Debit Merch Inv, Credit I/S. Decrease: Debit I/S, Credit Merch Inv.
  • Accrued Revenue: Debit Accounts Receivable, Credit Revenue (for services performed but not yet billed)

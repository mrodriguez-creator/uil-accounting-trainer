Payroll Reference Guide

Key formulas and rates used on UIL tests:

GROSS PAY
Regular Pay = Hours (up to 40) × Rate
Overtime Pay = OT Hours × Rate × 1.5
Gross Pay = Regular + Overtime
Commission = Sales × Rate%
Piece Rate = Units × Per-Unit Rate
EMPLOYER TAX RATES
Social Security: 6.2% (cap varies)
Medicare: 1.45% (no cap)
FUTA: 0.6%–0.8% (cap $7,000)
SUTA: 1.1%–2.7% (cap $9,000)
FIT — Always "Disregard"
ENTRY CLASSIFICATION (SE-E / PTE-R)
A = Salary Expense Entry: SS Pay, Med Pay, FIT Pay, SIT Pay, Net Pay, employee deductions
B = Payroll Tax Expense Entry: SS Pay, Med Pay, FUTA Pay, SUTA Pay
C = Both Entries: FICA taxes (SS + Medicare appear in both)
